# Iqraʾ

A Qurʾānic Arabic learning app: letters and tajwīd lessons, every sūrah with colour-coded tajwīd and the meaning of every word, a ḥifẓ (memorisation) planner, reviews, and a garden that grows with your XP.

Open it at **https://emcd21.github.io/Iqra/**.

## Install it on an iPhone

1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open Iqraʾ from its icon. After the first launch it works offline.

Your progress is saved on the phone. To bring progress over from another copy of Iqraʾ, open **Profile → Move your progress** there and tap **Copy progress code**, then in this app tap **Paste a code**.

## Sources

- Qurʾān text: Uthmani script, Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim (Madinah muṣḥaf), via the Noble Qurʾān Encyclopedia (`quran-json`); verse meanings: Saheeh International.
- Word meanings: Quran.com's English word-by-word translation.
- Pages and juzʾ: `quran-meta`.
- Fonts: Amiri Quran, Scheherazade New, Source Serif 4 and Inter, under the SIL Open Font License.
